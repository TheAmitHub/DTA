1. Website information & Scope

This website introduces DTA Digital Services and its virtual assistant, content, video editing, SEO, and web development support.

2. Enquiries and service engagements

Submitting an enquiry via our website or connecting through platforms like Upwork does not create an automatic contract. The scope, fees, schedule, and deliverables for paid work are confirmed via agency email (hello@digitaltechassist.com) or official freelance platform channels before work begins.

3. Payment Milestones & Structure

To ensure smooth project execution and professional commitment, DTA follows a structured milestone payment policy for all service engagements:

  • Initial Deposit (20%): Payable upfront before the project commences to secure slot allocation and resource planning.
  • Mid-Work Milestone (50%): Payable at the middle stage of the project or upon completion of major foundational work.
  • Final Check & Delivery (30%): Payable when the work is fully ready for final review and testing, prior to the final handover of source files or project deployment.

Note: All payments and financial transactions are coordinated securely via approved agency channels or freelance platforms. No direct payment gateways are processed on this website.

4. Project Cancellation, Stoppage & Refund Policy

We value mutual respect, clear communication, and professional patience. If a client decides to halt or cancel a project midway, the following terms apply:

  • Valid Reason & Proof: Refunds or partial adjustments are only considered if there is a verified, valid reason backed by proper professional justification and mutual discussion.
  • 50% Return Policy: In case of a valid cancellation request with acceptable proof, DTA will review the completed work hours/effort and may return up to 50% of the currently paid/non-utilized project amount.
  • No-Return Conditions: If cancellation occurs without a valid professional reason, or due to unresponsiveness, breach of respectful communication, or absence of valid proof, no refund will be issued, and all prior payments made will remain non-refundable to cover allocated resources and time.

5. Client responsibilities

Clients agree to provide accurate project materials, timely feedback, and maintain professional patience and respectful communication throughout the engagement. You are responsible for the rights associated with any media supplied to DTA.

6. Intellectual property

Unless agreed otherwise in writing, website branding, text, and design assets are protected. Ownership and usage rights for commissioned client work are formally transferred only upon completion of all agreed payment milestones.

Projects may involve third-party platforms (hosting providers, social networks, SEO tools, Upwork). Their policies and uptime are governed by those respective platforms. Digital marketing and SEO results depend on variable external factors; thus, specific organic search rankings or algorithmic outcomes are not explicitly guaranteed unless stated in a separate written agreement.

We may update these terms as our agency services evolve. The latest version will always be posted on this page with a revised review date.

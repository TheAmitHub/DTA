Privacy policy.

Last reviewed: 25 October 2026

1. Who we are

DTA Digital Services (“DTA”, “we”, “us”) provides practical virtual assistant support, content updates, video and reels editing, YouTube/Instagram captions and titles, YouTube shorts editing, SEO-related optimization, and small website development projects. Website: digitaltechassist.com.

2. Information you provide & Communication channels

If you contact us through our website or connect via freelance platforms (such as Upwork), we may receive your name, email address, service requirements, project files, and any information you choose to share.

Important Note on Communication & Payments: All project discussions, information sharing, and coordination are conducted directly via our official agency email (hello@digitaltechassist.com) or through the respective freelance platform's secure messaging system. We do not process any payments, financial transactions, or credit card details directly through this website. Please do not send passwords, direct payment-card details, or sensitive financial data through general website contact forms.

3. Technical information

Our hosting provider and website tools may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request logs, and security events to maintain site performance and security.

4. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and discuss requested virtual assistant or digital support services via our agency mail.
  • To understand project specifications, manage content updates, edit reels/videos, write captions, perform SEO tasks, or build small websites as agreed.
  • To manage projects acquired through platforms like Upwork using the details and requirements collected directly from those platforms or direct agency communication.
  • To maintain website security, prevent spam, and troubleshoot technical errors.

5. Contact forms and spam protection

Our enquiry form uses Web3Forms and Cloudflare Turnstile for anti-abuse and spam protection. These providers process necessary technical data strictly to deliver their security services.

6. Cookies and local storage

This site avoids non-essential tracking by default. Local storage is not used to build advertising profiles or track visitors across other websites.

7. Data Sharing and Third-Party Platforms

We do not sell or rent enquiry information. Client details and project files shared via agency email or freelance platforms (like Upwork) are used solely for fulfilling the contracted digital work and are handled with strict confidentiality.

8. Retention and security

We retain correspondence and project data only as long as necessary to complete your tasks, maintain business records, or comply with legal obligations. While we apply standard security safeguards, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

9. Your choices and requests

You may contact us via our agency email to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have provided through our site.

10. Children's privacy

This website is intended for general business and professional service enquiries and is not designed to collect data from children.

11. External links

Our website may contain links to external platforms (such as Upwork, YouTube, Instagram, Medium). Their privacy practices are governed by their respective platform policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or operational methods. Updates will be published directly on this page.

13. Contact us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, reach out to us at hello@digitaltechassist.com .

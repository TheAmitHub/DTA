YOUR DATA. YOUR CONTROL.

Making your shared data safer is part of our work.

Some digital tasks require access to your website, social accounts or business tools. We treat that access carefully and only request what is needed for the agreed work. You stay in control of your accounts and permissions.

Use role-based accessWhere a platform supports it, invite us with the appropriate role instead of sharing your main password.
Never share OTPsWe do not need your one-time passwords or banking PINs to perform ordinary digital support.
Keep access limitedShare only relevant assets and permissions. Review or revoke access when the work ends.
Agree before we beginWe clarify responsibilities, deliverables and access requirements before starting a task.
A CLEAR WORKING PROCESS

From first conversation to finished work.

A simple process keeps expectations clear, protects access and gives both sides a chance to review the work.

  1. 01

    Understand your requirements

    We discuss your goals, current setup, priorities and the tasks you want support with.

  2. 02

    Define scope and next steps

    We confirm responsibilities, deliverables, timelines and costs before any work begins.

  3. 03

    Arrange safe access

    When access is needed, we guide you through the relevant platform permissions and request only what the work requires.

  4. 04

    Work, review and refine

    We carry out the agreed tasks, share progress where appropriate and make the revisions included in the agreed scope.

  5. 05

    Deliver and close responsibly

    You review the completed work. We hand over relevant files or updates and you can remove any access that is no longer required.

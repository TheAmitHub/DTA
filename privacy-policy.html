Privacy Policy & Use of Funds

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Privacy

This website shares my education journey and ways to offer support. Please contact me only with information you are comfortable sharing. Do not send passwords, banking credentials, full payment-card details, or other highly sensitive information through email or social media.

If you contact me by email or phone, I may use the details you provide to respond to your message or discuss guidance, mentorship, work, or educational opportunities. I will aim to keep such information private and will not knowingly publish personal contact details or private messages without permission, unless disclosure is required by law.

The hosting provider may process basic technical information, such as request logs, to deliver and secure the website. The site also stores your light/dark theme preference in your browser when browser storage is available. External services, such as the font provider, may process connection data according to their own policies.

If you have a privacy question or want to request deletion of information you have shared with me, contact hello@digitaltechassist.com. I will review reasonable requests, subject to any record-keeping or legal obligations that apply.

2. Payments & Financial Transactions

This website provides convenient buttons/links to support applications (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm) via UPI deep linking. This website does not process, handle, store, or collect any financial data, bank account numbers, UPI IDs, PINs, or transaction credentials.

When you click a payment button, it simply triggers your chosen third-party UPI application installed on your device. The entire process—including entering the amount, confirming details, entering your UPI PIN, and final payment authorization—is handled completely within your respective payment app and banking network under their own terms and privacy policies.

User Responsibility: Before completing any transfer or confirming a payment in your UPI application, please ensure that you independently verify the recipient's name, mobile number, and UPI handle displayed on your screen. I am not responsible for any errors, transaction failures, incorrect transfers, or issues arising from third-party payment applications.

3. Intended use of funds

Contributions received through this website are intended to support my continued education, learning resources, technical skills development, and related expenses that help me build a stronger foundation for my future.

If I become financially able to actively help others, I also hope to use some of my own resources to support school students with educational needs and learning opportunities. This is a future intention, not a promise that a fixed portion of current contributions will be distributed to others.

I will not knowingly use contributions for unrelated or improper purposes. I aim to handle support responsibly and share genuine progress updates where practical. Contributions are voluntary, and no particular educational, employment, or financial result is guaranteed.

4. Contact

For questions about this policy, the intended use of funds, or possible guidance and opportunities, contact me at hello@digitaltechassist.com or 9685028124.

EARLY LEARNING & CAREER DEVELOPMENT

Finding my direction

Coming from a Hindi-medium government school, learning computer programming was challenging at first, especially without access to enough technical resources. However, I never stopped learning. I worked hard to understand programming, explored online courses, and gained early work experience as a Junior Python Developer at a small company, Zedtech. This experience helped me realize that I needed to strengthen my skills further. Along the way, I also worked as an Office Executive at the DPS Regional Office and attended English communication classes to improve my confidence. My journey has had challenges, but I kept learning, gaining experience, and working toward a better future.

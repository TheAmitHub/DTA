GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered
clearly.

Here are a few things clients often want to know before starting.

Can I hire a Virtual Assistant part-time? +

Yes. We can discuss part-time or task-based support depending on the work, schedule and scope required.

Can you manage an existing YouTube or Instagram account? +

Yes. We can support eligible existing accounts with content research, planning, publishing assistance, audience communication and ongoing account tasks. Results depend on the account, content and audience; growth is never guaranteed.

Do you set up Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp Business? +

We can help set up and organise business profiles, connect relevant Meta tools, prepare content and support ongoing management. Account ownership remains with you.

Can you update the content on my existing website? +

Yes. We can help revise text, service descriptions, page content, images and other agreed website updates. Access and scope are confirmed before work begins.

What is included in the ₹2,999 small-business website offer? +

It is a starting price for a clearly scoped small website. Final pricing depends on page count, content, features, integrations and revisions. Domain, hosting, premium themes, paid plugins and third-party fees may be additional.

Do you run Meta Ads? Is the ad budget included? +

We can help set up, run and maintain agreed Meta campaigns. Advertising spend is paid separately by the client to Meta; our service fee is discussed before the campaign starts.

How do I share account access safely? +

Where possible, use official role-based access such as Meta Business Portfolio permissions, Google access invitations or platform collaborator roles. Do not send passwords or one-time codes through ordinary messages.

Can I request changes before final delivery? +

Yes. We include a review stage and handle revisions according to the scope agreed before work starts.
